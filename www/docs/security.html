Security & license

Understand where your workspace data is handled, what an approval controls, and how to report a problem. The controls described here have limits and do not guarantee that every task or integration is safe.

Account access Human approvals Data handling Applicable licenses

Workspace data and credentials

  • Hosted workspace. The Muster service stores and processes account and workspace records. Relevant task content can be sent to the model providers and connected services you use.
  • Provider keys. Keys saved through hosted account settings are held in an account-scoped encrypted vault on the service and used for provider requests. Settings reports whether a key is configured without returning its saved value.
  • Existing desktop installations. Local workspace and configuration data can remain on the computer running that installation. Using a local interface does not prevent external provider or app requests.
  • Companions and backups. Phones and watches can retain session data, drafts or caches. They are not full workspace backups. Review the export and restore behavior of your installed version; account sign-in and Drive consent do not prove that every record or credential can be recovered.

The privacy policy describes data handling. Account & device setup explains the distinction between an account, a provider connection, device permission and backup consent.

Action controls and their limits

Supported engines surface permission requests as approval cards. Read the proposed action and destination before responding. Standing grants apply within their configured scope; engine and tool behavior can differ. An approval record does not establish that the resulting work is correct.

Configured budgets and available usage reports help you review consumption. Provider reporting can be incomplete or delayed, and connected services can bill separately. Privacy Shield can mask recognized patterns on supported paths; it is not a guarantee that all sensitive content is detected or kept on your device.

Task receipts retain available activity and usage information. Signature verification checks a supported receipt's integrity; it does not prove task quality or comprehensive security. See Approvals & privacy for the controls in the conversation.

Reporting a security issue

Use the private reporting contact in security.txt. Include the affected version and steps to reproduce, and omit real provider keys, session credentials and personal task content. Do not publish exploit details in a public support thread.
