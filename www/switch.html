Coming from Skales

Bring your Agent Skills (SKILL.md) — Muster reads the same format. Your goals become routines (cron-precision schedules), and your Cockpit view maps to Muster's roster + Automations. What you gain: separate teammate identities instead of persona-switching, a shared Brain every bot reads, and Vaultgram backups of everything.

Coming from OpenClaw or Hermes

Your MEMORY.md files carry over directly — each Muster bot keeps its own memory in exactly that format. Point your old working folders at new bots during onboarding; Project Scout suggests a full team lineup from any codebase automatically.

Coming from Sintra

Sintra's twelve helpers cost $97/mo and can't see each other's work. In Muster, teammates share one room, one context, one approval flow — and receipts for every job. Self-host free and compare before you pay anyone anything.

How to switch

  1. Download Muster — one installer, no Docker, no terminal.
  2. Add your first teammate — or hit Quick start and get working in under a minute.
  3. Hire a finished team — 15 packs in the library replace weeks of prompt-tuning.
  4. Turn on Vaultgram — your Google account backed up, encrypted, into your own Telegram channel.
