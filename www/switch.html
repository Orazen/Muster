Coming from Skales

Bring your Agent Skills (SKILL.md) — Muster reads the same format. Set up scheduled work as routines and find your bots in the roster + Automations. Muster gives each bot its own identity, with a shared Brain and optional Vaultgram backups for the files you choose.

Coming from OpenClaw or Hermes

Your MEMORY.md files carry over directly — each Muster bot keeps its own memory in exactly that format. Point your old working folders at new bots during onboarding; Project Scout suggests a full team lineup from any codebase automatically.

Coming from Sintra

Start with one recurring job and create the specialist bots it needs. Add them to a shared room, supply your team context, and review their output and task receipts. Try the workflow in the workspace Muster hosts, using your own model provider.

ONE TASK TO BEGIN WITH

Make the first move a small one.

  1. Choose your workspace. Open Muster in your browser, or download the desktop app for work on your computer.
  2. Add your first teammate. Connect your model provider, give the bot context, and choose a task you can check.
  3. Build out the team. Browse the team library and review the members, tools and routines before importing a pack.
  4. Bring over the files you need. Follow the setup guide for backup and Drive connections.
