Coming from Skales

Bring your Agent Skills (SKILL.md) — Muster reads the same format. Set up scheduled work as routines and find your bots in the roster + Automations. Muster gives each bot its own identity, with a shared Brain and optional Vaultgram backups for the files you choose.

Coming from OpenClaw or Hermes

Your MEMORY.md files carry over directly — each Muster bot keeps its own memory in exactly that format. Point your old working folders at new bots during onboarding; Project Scout suggests a full team lineup from any codebase automatically.

Coming from Sintra

Start with one recurring job and create the specialist bots it needs. Add them to a shared room, supply your team context, and review their output and task receipts. Try the workflow on your own server before choosing a hosted plan.

How to switch

  1. Download Muster — one installer, no Docker, no terminal.
  2. Add your first teammate — or hit Quick start and get working in under a minute.
  3. Hire a finished team — 15 packs in the library replace weeks of prompt-tuning.
  4. Set up Vaultgram — pair your Telegram channel, then back up files, sync Google Drive, or import an extracted Google Takeout archive.
