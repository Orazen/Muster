Coding agents

Your terminal agents, promoted to teammates

Claude Code, Codex, and their cousins are brilliant and stranded — one conversation, one terminal, gone when you close the laptop. Muster gives each one a name, a desk, and a job list that survives the weekend.

⌨️

Drive them from anywhere

Assign a task from your phone, the web console, or the terminal. The agent works on its own machine with its own credentials — not inside your terminal session.

🖥️

Each agent gets a desk

Choose an available computer for a supported engine. Separate workspaces can keep tasks apart; computer availability and isolation depend on your configuration.

🚦

Approvals, not vibes

Supported tool actions can pause at approval cards. Review the proposed action and your bot’s permissions before granting broader access.

🧾

Receipts per task

Review a task's recorded turns and usage, with cost when the engine reports it. Copy the receipt into a standup note alongside the result.

🔁

Routines on a schedule

Schedule repeatable work such as a weekly draft or a review checklist. The connected host and provider must remain available for a routine to run.

👥

Delegation between bots

Ask a supported teammate to delegate or review another bot’s output. Keep the discussion and your final decision together in the workspace.

Know what you’re connecting

Clear boundaries.
Decisions you can see.

Your workspace, its tools and your backups have different jobs. Here is what to expect today.

Know where work lives

The web workspace is stored on Muster’s server. Desktop workspaces are stored on your computer. Google sign-in does not merge these copies.

Connect deliberately

Model providers and connected apps receive requests when used. Review their permissions and terms, and your bot’s approval settings.

Back up with care

Desktop backups require setup and a recovery passphrase. Connecting Drive alone does not start automatic backups or sync. Web-to-device recovery is still in development.

Privacy Shield is optional masking for supported text flows, not a guarantee that all sensitive information is filtered. Product analytics can be switched off in Settings. Read the privacy policy →

A few things
worth knowing.

What can I use Muster for?
Give a named assistant a task, discuss a plan, and review its results. Start with research, organizing work, or drafting a reply. Available tools depend on the model, connections and computer you choose.
Is Muster free?
Muster is free during the beta. Bring your own provider key or use a model available in your account. Provider usage and optional computer services may have their own charges. Future pricing has not been decided.
How are the web and desktop apps different?
The web app opens your hosted workspace in a browser. The desktop app runs a workspace on your computer and can use supported local engines and tools. Signing in on both does not automatically merge their workspaces.
Where does my data live?
The web workspace is stored on Muster’s server. The desktop workspace is stored on your computer. Connecting Google Drive grants storage access; it does not by itself start backups or sync. Desktop backup and restore need separate setup. Automatic web-to-device recovery is still being developed.
Can I use Muster on my phone or Watch?
iPhone, Android and Apple Watch companions are in beta development. They connect to a running Muster host; they do not run desktop engines themselves. Check the download page for current installation and testing availability.
Which models can I connect?
Muster supports multiple model providers, compatible API endpoints and local engines. Desktop engines use their installed accounts; web providers use the keys connected to your account. Tools, vision and computer access vary by driver and model.
What does Privacy Shield cover?
Privacy Shield is optional masking for supported API-driver text. When enabled for a compatible bot, it masks recognized patterns before that text is sent and records masking counts. It is not a guarantee that every secret, attachment or provider request is filtered. Review sensitive content and your provider’s terms.
Does the app send analytics?
Muster can send product-usage events and check for updates. You can turn usage analytics off in Settings. Models, connected apps and other online features send requests to their services when used. See the privacy policy for details.
