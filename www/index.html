Product

Agents that behave like a team

One assistant in one box is the wrong shape for agents. Muster treats AI the way a real team works — a roster you chat with like contacts, each with its own personality, memory, model, computer, and connected apps.

🧠

A brain per bot

A model picker with a provider rail, side by side. Swap a bot's model mid-conversation — the cheap one triages, the smart one writes.

🖥️

Every bot gets a computer

Give each bot a real cloud Linux desktop it drives while you watch live, or point it straight at your own Mac.

🙋

Approvals that hold

Shell commands, file edits, and questions surface as inline cards — Allow, Deny, or answer in the thread.

🔌

500+ connected apps

Gmail, Slack, GitHub, Notion, Linear and hundreds more through Composio. OAuth once, reuse across the roster.

🤝

Bots work together

Groups, group calls, and delegation. Import a whole roster from the team library and put it to work.

🔑

Keys once, local always

Credentials persist in ~/.muster and the fleet hot-reloads. Secrets stay write-only.

Local-first

The computer is yours

One small harness server on 127.0.0.1 owns every agent process. Transcripts, keys, and events live in ~/.muster — not in a cloud, not behind a seat count.

Any model, your key

Bots run on the AI CLIs already installed and logged in on your machine. Your existing subscriptions, no proxy in the middle.

Nothing phones home

No telemetry pipeline, no account required to run it. The harness only talks to the engines you point it at.

An audit log you own

Every action a bot takes lands in a local event log you can read, grep, and keep. Plain files, not a dashboard.

Engines

Bring your own model

Muster does not ship a model of its own. Your bots run on the AI CLIs already installed on your machine, using your existing logins. Point any engine at a custom binary in Settings → Engines.
