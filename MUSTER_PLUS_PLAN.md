# Muster+ — The World's Best AI Assistant

## Executive Summary

Muster+ is a privacy-first, viral-friendly AI assistant that combines the best features from Vellum, GAIA, OpenMausBot, and other leading AI platforms into one cohesive product.

### What Makes Muster+ Different

Unlike ChatGPT or Claude (which are closed and data-hungry), Muster+ is:
- Privacy-first: Your data stays on your device by default
- Universal: 200+ AI models through one API key
- Proactive: Does work for you automatically
- Viral: Built-in sharing mechanics
- Open source: Fully customizable

## Vision Statement

To create the first AI assistant that everyone uses daily because:
1. It respects their privacy (zero data collection by default)
2. It's easy to use (no technical knowledge needed)
3. It's proactive (does work for you, not just when you ask)
4. It's viral (easy to share with friends and earn rewards)
5. It has super-memory (remembers everything across 8 memory types)
